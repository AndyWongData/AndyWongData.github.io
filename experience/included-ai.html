Work Experience

included.ai

Data Scientist

December 2023 — June 2024

01 — Overview

At included.ai, I worked on a graduate capstone project focused on helping organizations approach diversity, equity, and inclusion goals with data. The project combined predictive modeling, interactive visualization, and user-centered design to turn organizational hiring data into a tool that could help professionals understand where their workforce was headed and explore potential goals.

02 — What I do

This project gave me the opportunity to work across both the analytical and product sides of data science. Rather than treating the model as the final product, I focused on how the analysis could become something a person could actually understand and use.

I worked through the process of turning an open-ended question into a measurable problem, exploring how historical hiring movement could be used to understand future trends, and finding a clear way to communicate those results.

I also thought carefully about the experience around the analysis—what a user would need to see, what information would be useful, and how the interface could make the goal-setting process feel less complicated.

View the final product

03 — Tools & Technologies

Python Predictive Modeling Data Visualization Interactive Visualization UI / UX

04 — Impact

The projection algorithm was able to predict hiring trends with 90% accuracy, giving users a data-driven way to think about how their organization's workforce might change over time.

The interactive visualization increased user retention time by 35%, helping turn what could have been a difficult analytical problem into something more engaging and understandable.

The final product brought the modeling, visualization, and interface together into a single DEI goal-setting experience designed for professionals working with organizational workforce data.

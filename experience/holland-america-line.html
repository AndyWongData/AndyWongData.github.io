Work Experience

Holland America Line

Data Scientist, Targeted Marketing

February 2025 — Ongoing

01 — Overview

At Holland America Line and Seabourn, I work on using data and machine learning to personalize the guest experience and drive onboard revenue. My work spans the full lifecycle of an initiative—from forming a hypothesis and building the underlying data models to deploying recommendations, designing experiments, monitoring results, and communicating findings to leadership. I also support the broader organization through revenue forecasting, reporting, and the development of marketing materials.

02 — What I do

My work usually starts with a question: who should we reach, what should we show them, and how do we know if it worked?

I work across the process of answering those questions. Sometimes that means building a model to understand guest behavior. Other times, it means designing an experiment, figuring out how to measure its results, forecasting where revenue is headed, or building the reporting and marketing tools needed to put an idea into practice.

A big part of my role is also connecting these pieces. I turn raw data into something a marketing team can use, then use the results to learn what to do next. That might mean writing SQL and Python, building a Power BI report, creating an HTML marketing asset, or working with stakeholders to decide whether an initiative should be scaled or changed.

I also spend time making the work around me more reliable and repeatable—whether that's improving shared code, monitoring production processes, or helping an intern take an idea from a model into something the business can actually use.

03 — Tools & Technologies

Python SQL Snowflake PowerBI HTML Machine Learning A/B Testing & Experimentation Revenue Forecasting

04 — Impact

The work I do is ultimately tied to helping Holland America Line and Seabourn make better decisions about how to engage guests and grow onboard revenue.

One of my largest initiatives, Shorex Positive Surprise, uses machine learning to score guests mid-voyage based on their likelihood to purchase shore excursions. The resulting automation increased take rate by 10% and revenue per port day by 5%.

Beyond individual models, I have helped build the infrastructure around experimentation and personalization so that ideas can be tested, measured, and scaled across the business. My forecasting and reporting work gives the Future Cruise team a consistent view of revenue performance and the targets they are working toward, while shared engineering tools and monitoring help ensure that production automations continue to run reliably.
